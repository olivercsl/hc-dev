Questions

Questions we get asked

The practical ones, answered plainly. If yours is not here, ask us.

Can you take over our existing AWS accounts?

Yes. It is an administrative change, not a rebuild. Billing moves under AWS Organizations and management moves to us, while your workloads, data and architecture stay exactly where they are. No downtime, nothing to re-deploy.

What changes if we buy Microsoft licences through you?

We become the Cloud Solution Provider on your subscriptions, so licences, renewals and seat changes come through one Australian team. Your tenant, data and users stay where they are, and we take on administration and the security baseline.

Do we need to migrate our Microsoft 365 tenant?

No. Moving to Harbour Cloud transfers billing and administration only. Nothing is rebuilt, no mailboxes move, and your people notice nothing on the day it happens.

Which Microsoft 365 plan do we need, Business Premium, E3 or E5?

It depends on how your people actually work. Business Premium already includes Defender, Intune, Entra ID and Purview for most smaller firms, while E3 and E5 suit larger tenants and stricter compliance obligations. We review what you own against what you use, then right-size it before renewal.

How do you keep Azure and AWS costs under control?

Cost governance is part of the build rather than an afterthought: budgets, alerts, tagging, right-sized resources and commitment planning, plus a Well-Architected review on AWS. You can see where the spend goes and why.

Are we ready for Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Copilot surfaces whatever a person can already reach, so oversharing and stale permissions are the usual blockers. We run a readiness review across permissions, sensitivity labels and licensing first, then switch it on.

Can AI run on our own infrastructure instead of a public cloud?

Yes. For data that cannot leave your premises we design and run private, on-premises inference clusters on open-source models, then build the same agentic workloads on top. Everything stays on your network.

How quickly can you start?

Quickly. We are a small senior team, so discovery usually starts within days, and a proof of concept on your own data follows in weeks rather than quarters.
